About the Club

The Green Trails Club was founded in 2022 by a group of students who wanted to spend more time outdoors and explore the natural beauty of Grenada. The club started with only 8 members, but it slowly grew as more students became interested in hiking and outdoor activities.

The main goal of the club is to encourage young people to explore nature while also learning how to take care of the environment.

What the Club Does

The Green Trails Club organizes different activities throughout the year, including:

  • Hiking trips
  • Nature walks
  • Trail cleanups
  • Camping trips
  • Outdoor photography
  • Environmental awareness events
  • Beach and park cleanups
  • Sunrise hikes
  • Team-building activities
  • Educational nature tours

Club Mission

The mission of The Green Trails Club is to encourage people to explore nature, stay active, build friendships, and protect the environment.

Club Goals

  • Encourage students to become more active.
  • Help members discover new hiking trails.
  • Teach people about the environment.
  • Keep hiking trails clean.
  • Build teamwork and friendships.
  • Encourage responsible outdoor activities.

Club Rules

  1. Respect nature and wildlife.
  2. Stay with the group during hikes.
  3. Follow the hike leader's instructions.
  4. Do not litter.
  5. Respect other club members.
  6. Come prepared with water and suitable clothing.
  7. Take care of club equipment.
  8. Keep the trails clean.
  9. Make sure everyone feels welcome.
  10. Have fun and enjoy the outdoors!

Club Headquarters

The club's fictional headquarters is located in St. George’s, Grenada, near a small nature reserve. The building has a meeting room, equipment storage area, first-aid station, and a small garden where members grow plants.

Membership

Anyone between the ages of 15 and 25 can join the club. New members attend a short orientation where they learn about hiking safety, protecting nature, and the club's rules.

Membership fee: $25 EC per year.

Fun Fact

The Green Trails Club's first official hike had only 8 people, but they completed a 5-kilometre trail and ended the day with a picnic. The club now organizes larger hikes with members from different schools and communities.
